LEGO Parts Database

Common LEGO parts you can actually search by name. Nicknames, official catalog names, part numbers, and what we have in stock — all in one place.

Every LEGO builder has done it: you hold up a piece, you know exactly what it does, and you have no idea what it’s called. This database solves that. Each page shows the nickname builders use (cheese slope, jumper plate, headlight brick), the official BrickLink name (what the catalog actually files it under), the part number molded on the piece, and — the thing you really want — whether we have one in stock, in which color, at what price.

Starting with 20 of the most-searched parts in our catalog. For the full visual guide including quiz, read our post on naming every LEGO piece.
