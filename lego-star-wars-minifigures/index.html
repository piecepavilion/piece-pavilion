Authentic LEGO Star Wars minifigures

Star Wars is one of the most collected LEGO themes ever made, and the minifigures are a huge part of why. We sell only genuine LEGO Star Wars figures, each hand-inspected for completeness and condition. Star Wars figures carry catalog IDs beginning with "sw" — that code identifies the exact variant, since many characters were produced in multiple versions over the years.

Older and retired Star Wars figures can be hard to find and often appreciate in value, so they are popular with both builders and collectors.

Why buy Star Wars minifigures from us

We are a family-owned store with 100% positive BrickLink feedback. Every figure is checked before it ships and packed to protect the printing. Want the broader catalog? See all our LEGO minifigures, or read our minifigure collecting guide to learn how to identify and value any figure.

Yes — every figure is an authentic LEGO minifigure, never a counterfeit. We hand-inspect each one before shipping.

Each listing uses the exact BrickLink catalog ID (e.g., sw0028), which pins down the specific variant. The listing photo shows the actual figure type.
