Buy authentic LEGO minifigures with confidence

Every minifigure we sell is a genuine LEGO part — never a knock-off or counterfeit. We hand-inspect each figure for completeness and condition before it ships, so what you see is exactly what arrives. Whether you are completing a collection, finishing a set, or hunting a specific character, you are buying from a family-owned store that builds right alongside you.

New to collecting? Start with our guide on how to start collecting LEGO minifigures for the types, catalog IDs, and how to spot fakes.

How minifigures are identified and priced

Every LEGO minifigure has a unique BrickLink catalog ID — for example, Star Wars figures start with "sw", Super Heroes with "sh", and blind-bag Collectible Minifigures with "col". That ID is how you identify any figure and check its value. Rarity, theme, age, and condition all affect price, which is why retired and licensed figures often command more than common city figures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your LEGO minifigures authentic?

Yes. We only sell genuine LEGO minifigures and parts — never counterfeits. Each figure is hand-inspected for authenticity and condition before shipping.

Do minifigures come with their accessories?

Listings specify exactly what is included. If a figure comes with accessories, hat, or weapon, that is noted on the BrickLink listing. When in doubt, message us before ordering and we will confirm.

How fast do minifigures ship?

We pack and ship orders in 1–2 days. Minifigures are packed carefully to protect printing and prevent damage in transit.

Can you find a specific minifigure for me?

Often, yes. If you do not see the figure you want in stock, email us at piecepavilion@gmail.com with the name or catalog ID and we will let you know if we can source it.
