LEGO Black Friday 2026: Godzilla, Gifts and Deals

Piece Pavilion · November 26, 2026 · 5 min read

A LEGO Godzilla that stands more than two feet tall is a pretty good way to get people’s attention on Black Friday. But you don’t need to spend $450 to find something good. If you’re here for the monster, there’s a bonus build to look for. If you’re shopping for Christmas, there are a handful of real markdowns I’d check first.

Short on time? Check the Christmas Table Decoration for $23.99, the Williams F1 car for $55.99, and the Flower Arrangement for $65.99. Those were prices LEGO listed before the event opened, so recheck them before ordering. If you’re buying Godzilla, make sure its bonus is actually in your cart. LEGO’s U.S. Black Friday page

Godzilla: a new set, not a sale

LEGO Ideas Godzilla (21375) launches November 27 at $449.99 in the U.S. It has 5,363 pieces and stands over 27.5 inches tall with its Heat Ray attached. That's the regular launch price, not a markdown. If this is going on a shelf, I’d get out the tape measure before I got out the credit card.

The Godzilla Logo & Footprint (40888) is the part I’m most excited about. The 359-piece build has a blue Godzilla logo, a miniature skyline, and a tiny Mothra. The footprint is the exact size of the big model’s foot, and you can display it separately from the logo. It’s the kind of bonus that feels like it was actually designed for this set. LEGO’s reveal

To get it, your order has to include Godzilla itself. A different $450 LEGO order won’t qualify. The U.S. gift is scheduled for Nov. 27–30, while supplies last, with one per household. LEGO lists its value at $24.99, but you can’t buy it separately. Official U.S. gift terms

Quick note on the previous gifts

We’re treating the Retro Jukebox (40914) and Winter Snow Globe (40918) gifts as retired for this guide. LEGO’s posted terms still list a limited Nov. 27–29 offer while supplies last, but those terms don’t confirm what’s available in your cart. I wouldn’t spend extra expecting either gift. Check LEGO’s current U.S. offers.

Eight sale prices I’d check first

These discounts were listed on LEGO’s U.S. sale page before Black Friday opened. They’re a shortlist, not a promise that the prices or stock will hold through the weekend.

  • Eeyore: $10.39, was $12.99. The cheapest pick on the list and a good stocking stuffer for a Disney fan.
  • Carl, Russell & Kevin: $17.49, was $24.99. My under-$20 pick for anyone who loves Up.
  • Christmas Table Decoration: $23.99, was $39.99. At 40% off, this is the holiday deal I’d check first.
  • Bluey’s Family House with Memory Game: $48.99, was $69.99. A DUPLO option for a younger builder that also doubles as a game.
  • Williams Racing FW14B & Nigel Mansell: $55.99, was $79.99. My F1 pick for the car and the driver minifigure.
  • Enchanted Flying Ford Anglia: $63.99, was $79.99. A recognizable Harry Potter display piece without buying a whole castle.
  • Flower Arrangement: $65.99, was $109.99. At $44 off, it’s the biggest dollar saving on this shortlist.
  • Kakamora Barge: $69.99, was $99.99. The wild card if there’s a Moana fan on your list.

One last thing: LEGO Insiders can also check the rewards page for the Godzilla poster (3,000 points) and Mini Santa’s Workshop (2,400 points). Those are point redemptions, not extra gifts that come automatically with a purchase. My order of operations: buy what you wanted, check the discount, then make sure any gift you qualify for is in your cart. LEGO’s U.S. event and rewards page
