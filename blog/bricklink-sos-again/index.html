BrickLink SOS Again

Piece Pavilion · November 19, 2026 · 4 min read

Status checked November 19, 2026, at 6:04 p.m. Eastern

BrickLink is offline again. Its next update is due by Friday, November 20, at 1 p.m. Eastern. There is no confirmed reopening time.

The blue parrot is back. On Thursday evening, opening BrickLink sent us to its recovery page instead of the marketplace. BrickLink says it is investigating “unusual activity.” It hasn’t said what it detected, whether any member data was affected this time, or whether this is connected to the earlier November outage. BrickLink service update

The timeline

All times are Eastern. The times below are for published updates, not necessarily the first moment the site went down.

WhenWhat we can verify
November 19, early morning (reported)In a November 19 discussion, sellers recalled an earlier scheduled-maintenance notice. One recalled a 1–2 a.m. window without specifying a time zone. We couldn’t verify the original notice or whether the maintenance finished. Community reports
November 19, noonBrickLink posted that the site was offline while it investigated an issue, with another update due that afternoon. Official notice, under Previous Updates
November 19, later updateBrickLink said it would remain offline while it investigated unusual activity. It linked a new FAQ and set Friday’s update deadline. Official notice
November 20, by 1 p.m.Next promised status update: 10 a.m. Pacific, 18:00 UTC, or 7 p.m. Central European Time. BrickLink has not promised service will resume then. Official notice and FAQ

Could the reported maintenance be connected?

The timing is worth asking about. Community reports point to the early hours of Thursday, November 19, rather than establishing a Wednesday, November 18 start. One commenter recalled a 1–2 a.m. window but gave no time zone. We couldn’t verify that this was the monthly maintenance, its exact timing, or that the scheduled work took place. The screenshot here shows the later service update, not the original maintenance notice.

If maintenance did take place, BrickLink could have noticed a separate issue during its checks, or the two events could have been unrelated. We don’t have evidence that a maintenance change triggered the outage. To establish a link, BrickLink would need to say what it found, when it found it, and whether that happened during or after the scheduled work. Community discussion · BrickLink service update

Could this be a hack?

It’s possible. BrickLink says it is investigating unusual activity and is keeping the site offline while it works to restore service safely. That leaves a security incident on the table, but it doesn’t tell us whether someone got in, whether the site was taken down as a precaution, or whether any member data was affected. BrickLink hasn’t identified an attacker, a method, or a link to the earlier shutdown. The parrot screen can’t answer any of those questions. BrickLink service update

What this means for buyers and sellers

The hardest part for a seller isn’t refreshing the homepage. It’s having a packing table full of parts and not being able to open the order to check what belongs in the box. BrickLink’s FAQ confirms that neither buyers nor sellers can access or update orders through the site while it’s unavailable. The company says it intends to exclude downtime from relevant response-time, feedback-window and vacation-mode calculations. It also warns that automated messages can arrive late or reflect activity you couldn’t complete while the site was down. BrickLink’s order and store guidance

  • Buyers: Save your confirmation and payment receipt. If checkout stopped halfway, verify the original order and payment before paying again or ordering a duplicate. The outage alone doesn’t tell you whether your order was placed or shipped.
  • Sellers: Keep any existing pick lists, inventory exports and tracking records. If you can’t verify the order contents, shipping address or payment status from records you already have, set the order aside. When access returns, reconcile offline work before retrying uploads or changing order statuses in bulk.
  • Studio users: BrickLink says you can still edit local model files and make instructions. Online-only My Studio files, price and color lookup, and Gallery features depend on the connection and may not work correctly. Studio guidance

On Friday, we’re looking for three answers: whether BrickLink can give a reopening estimate, whether it has guidance for open orders, and whether it can explain the timing of the maintenance and the outage. Until then, the live service notice is the best place to check.

One note about last week

This outage comes about a week after BrickLink reopened from its earlier November shutdown. BrickLink described the earlier event as a cyber incident; it hasn’t said whether the two outages are related. Read the previous SOS update.
