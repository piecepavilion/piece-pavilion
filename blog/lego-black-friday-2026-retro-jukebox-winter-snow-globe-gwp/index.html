LEGO Black Friday Gifts With Purchase: Retro Jukebox vs. Winter Snow Globe

Piece Pavilion · November 24, 2026 · 3 min read

If your LEGO wish list already adds up to $170, I’d aim for the Winter Snow Globe and call it a day. The Retro Jukebox needs $250 in qualifying purchases. That’s an $80 difference, and the jukebox isn’t enough reason to add $80 of sets you didn’t want.

LEGO lists both U.S. gifts with purchase (GWP) for November 27–29, 2026, while supplies last. This is a Black Friday preview as of November 24, not a report that the gifts are in stock today. The earlier U.S. Insiders offers ran November 21–22. And although LEGO’s bigger Black Friday event goes through Cyber Monday, these two gifts are listed only through Sunday.

Winter Snow Globe (40918): $170 in qualifying LEGO merchandise

The Snow Globe is my pick for a cart that’s already close to $170. It’s the one I’d put out with the holiday builds, and it asks less of your budget. LEGO lists an approximate retail value of $24.99, but that’s not a cash discount on your order.

There’s an important exception: Godzilla (21375) is excluded from this offer. If that’s the set you’re buying, don’t count on its price to qualify you for the Snow Globe. Check the cart instead of assuming the total on screen is the qualifying amount.

Retro Jukebox (40914): $250 in qualifying LEGO merchandise

On looks alone, I’d pick the jukebox. I can picture it tucked into a diner or beside a record shop in a LEGO city, which gives me more reasons to keep it out after December. LEGO puts its approximate retail value at $29.99. If you’re already buying $250 of qualifying LEGO anyway, it’s a fun extra. If your list stops at $170, that extra $80 buys actual sets, not just the upgrade from one free build to the other.

Can one order get both gifts?

A $250 qualifying cart could meet the two listed spending thresholds, but the published terms don’t guarantee both gifts on every order. Stock and exclusions still matter. Look for both gift lines in your bag before you pay. If only one shows up, I wouldn’t count on the other arriving in the box.

Godzilla has its own separate offer, the Godzilla Logo & Footprint (40888), listed for November 27–30 with a qualifying Godzilla purchase, while supplies last. That one has a different rule and an extra day. Don’t use its dates to plan around the Snow Globe or Jukebox.

What I’d check before placing the order

  1. Build the cart from sets you actually planned to buy. If you’re a few dollars short, add something you’ll use rather than a random set.
  2. Check qualifying LEGO merchandise, not just the cart total. Standard Pick a Brick orders are excluded from both offers.
  3. Confirm the gift you want appears in your bag before checking out. LEGO lists these offers online, in LEGO Stores and through LEGO Catalog phone orders, with one of each offer per household.

LEGO says you can’t apply these promotions to an earlier purchase, and if you return the qualifying purchase for a refund, the gift goes back too. Check LEGO’s U.S. Black Friday terms before you pay, and check the earlier Insiders terms if you’re comparing against a screenshot from last weekend.

My call: buy the sets you came for. If the Snow Globe falls into the bag at $170, great. If the Jukebox is there on a cart you were already taking to $250, even better.
