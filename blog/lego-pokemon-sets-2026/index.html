LEGO Pokémon Is Here: The 2026 Sets — and How to Build Your Own From Parts

Piece Pavilion · July 17, 2026

Two of the biggest names in the world just teamed up: on July 7, LEGO and The Pokémon Company officially revealed the first wave of LEGO Pokémon sets. Pre-orders are already open, the first sets land August 1, 2026, and yes — Pikachu and Eevee are getting the brick treatment. Here's the full lineup, and a fun way to join in right now without waiting for release day.

What's coming August 1

The launch wave is built for display builders and fans alike, headlined by three buildable Pokémon:

  • Munchlax — 757 pieces, $69.99
  • Arcanine — 1,190 pieces, $109.99
  • Rayquaza — 1,083 pieces, $129.99

Alongside those, LEGO is rolling out a range of smaller SMART Play sets and figures — including the two everyone was waiting for, Pikachu and Eevee, plus Pokémon Trainer minifigures. Looking further ahead, an Iconic Trainer Moments Poké Ball and an up-scaled Red minifigure arrive in October. If you want any of these brand-new, LEGO's own shop is the place to pre-order.

You don't have to wait — build your own today

Here's the best part of the hobby: you don't need an official set (or $130) to put a Pokémon on your shelf. With the right colors, standard bricks and plates make brilliant Pokémon builds — and buying just the parts you need is exactly what our store is for. A few ideas to get building this weekend:

  • Build a Pikachu. Yellow is the whole game here — stack yellow bricks for the body, add black tips for the ears and tail, red cheeks, and a couple of round plates for eyes. We keep dozens of yellow parts in stock.
  • Build a Poké Ball. The most iconic sphere in gaming is just three colors: red up top, white on the bottom, and a black band with a single stud in the middle. A satisfying afternoon build.
  • Build your favorite starter. Bulbasaur greens, Charmander oranges, Squirtle blues — pick your Pokémon and pull the exact colors to match. Search any color in our Product Finder and see what we've got.

Why parts beat sets for fan builds

Official sets are great, but a custom build lets you make your Pokémon, at your size, for a fraction of the price. Instead of paying set money for pieces you'll never use, you buy only the bricks the build calls for — and you get to be the designer. Our Product Finder searches our live inventory by color, part, or number, so you can round up everything a build needs in a few clicks. New to designing your own? Our summer building ideas are a fun place to start.

And if it's the Trainer minifigures that caught your eye, minifigs are our specialty too — browse our minifigure collection to build out your own cast of characters.

However you catch 'em — pre-ordering the big sets or building your own from a bin of bricks — this is a fun summer to be a Pokémon fan. Pick your Pokémon, grab your colors, and start building.
