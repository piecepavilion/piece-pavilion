LEGO October 2026 Releases: The Sets and Freebies Worth Grabbing Before the Holiday Rush

Piece Pavilion · October 31, 2026 · 4 min read

October 2026 is a light month for brand-new LEGO lines and a heavy one for small, easy-to-miss releases: a Pick a Brick refill, a free gift-with-purchase, and a handful of Icons and Ideas sets that will be on gift lists by December. Here is what is actually on the October calendar, based on release listings published this month.

Three sets worth knowing about: Bookshop, Holiday House, Wallace & Gromit

New Elementary's October 2026 roundup lists three sets that matter for gift buyers: LEGO Icons 11379 Bookshop: Book Nook, LEGO Icons 11387 Holiday House, and LEGO Ideas 21371 Wallace & Gromit. Two are Icons sets aimed at adult builders, and the third is a fan-designed Ideas set.

The Holiday House is the one to watch. Seasonal Icons sets tend to sell through before the holidays, and anything that sells through in November turns into a secondary-market purchase in January. We are not quoting prices or piece counts here because the listings we could verify did not include them. Check LEGO.com for current numbers before you plan a budget around any of these.

The free one: Astro Bot (40899) gift-with-purchase

The same roundup lists 40899 Astro Bot as an October gift-with-purchase, with a release window of October 1 to 7, 2026, while stocks last. The spend threshold was not in the source we pulled, so confirm it on LEGO.com. The window is the point: gift-with-purchase sets run out of stock quickly, and a small polybag-style set with a game tie-in is exactly the kind of item that shows up used a few weeks later.

Pick a Brick got over 800 new pieces on October 1

New Elementary reports that more than 800 pieces were added to LEGO Pick a Brick on Thursday, October 1, 2026. If you build MOCs or want spare parts for repairs, a refill is the best time to buy because popular colors drain fast. If a part you need is gone, that is what BrickLink and our used parts inventory are for.

Marvel and Avengers: Doomsday: four sets, reported for October 4

The same listings say LEGO Super Heroes will release four Marvel sets tied to the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday movie on October 4. We could not verify set numbers, prices, or the exact lineup, so treat the specifics as unconfirmed. Movie-tie-in sets usually get the most attention near the film's release, and minifigures from them tend to be the first pieces collectors chase.

What is still rumor

StoneWars' earlier roundups pointed to Pokémon sets 72154 and 40868 for October, and a French site's October list includes 72306 LEGO PlayStation. Those came from rumor coverage, not official announcements, so wait for LEGO to confirm before you plan around them. Brick Fanatics also published a guide to every set launching in November 2026 on October 25. We will cover it once its details are confirmed.

Bottom line

If you only do one thing this month, check whether the Holiday House and the Astro Bot gift-with-purchase are still available, since both are likely to run out before the holiday rush. Everything else on this list can wait, and so can any rumored set.
