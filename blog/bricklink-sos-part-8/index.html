BrickLink SOS Part 8

Piece Pavilion · November 20, 2026 · 2 min read

BrickLink is aiming for Tuesday. Here’s what we know.

If you’ve been checking BrickLink between sorting parts or waiting for an order, the blue parrot is still there. But Friday’s update finally gave us a date: BrickLink is aiming to reopen Tuesday, November 24. There’s no opening hour, and Tuesday depends on how the investigation goes and whether the team can bring the site back safely. The next thing to watch is Monday’s update, due by 1 p.m. Eastern on November 23.

How we got here

  • Thursday, November 19, noon ET: BrickLink said the marketplace was offline while it looked into an issue affecting the site.
  • Thursday, November 19, 1 p.m. ET: BrickLink said it was investigating “unusual activity” and would stay offline while that work continued.
  • Friday, November 20: BrickLink named Tuesday as its reopening target and said it would share another status update Monday.

What about orders already in the system?

The updated BrickLink FAQ makes a useful distinction: if a seller had already processed and packed an order, it may still ship. If the order hadn’t been processed, it might have to wait until the marketplace comes back. You can’t look up or update orders through BrickLink right now, so if something genuinely can’t wait, look for the buyer’s or seller’s email address in the original order confirmation or shipping email.

For sellers, BrickLink says it plans to leave the outage out of its response-time, feedback-window and vacation-mode calculations. You might still get an automated notice, including one about store inactivity. BrickLink says you don’t need to act on a notice clearly tied to something you couldn’t do while the site was down.

Why is the whole site down?

BrickLink says its services are closely connected, so sometimes it has to take the whole site offline to work safely. It’s rebuilding the platform so unaffected services can eventually stay up when another part needs work. That explains why there may not be a partially open marketplace, but it doesn’t tell us what triggered this shutdown. BrickLink hasn’t confirmed a hack or linked the outage to the reported maintenance.

In the meantime, you can still build and make instructions in Studio using models saved on your computer. Models stored only in My Studio and online tools like the price guide are still out of reach. If you’ve got a Designer Program order, that’s separate: LEGO.com fulfills those orders, and BrickLink says the outage doesn’t affect them.
