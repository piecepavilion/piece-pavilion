LEGO Black Friday 2026: Dates, What's Confirmed, and What's Still Rumor

Piece Pavilion · October 30, 2026 · 3 min read

LEGO's 2026 Black Friday event runs Friday, November 27 through Cyber Monday, November 30. As of today, October 30, nothing about discounts or double points has been confirmed. Here is what is known, what is rumor, and how to plan around it.

The dates that matter

  • November 21-22: Insiders Weekend, the early window for LEGO Insiders members.
  • November 27: Black Friday. LEGO.com deals are expected to start at midnight. Third-party retailers often start earlier.
  • November 30: Cyber Monday, the last day of LEGO's event.

That is a nine-day stretch from Insiders Weekend through Cyber Monday. If you are buying gifts, you do not need to decide on the 27th.

What is confirmed, and what is not

LEGO has not announced its 2026 Black Friday discounts. It has not confirmed double Insiders points this year either. Anyone quoting a specific percentage off a specific set right now is guessing.

The one rumor getting traction is that the Black Friday gift-with-purchase or exclusive release will be the upcoming LEGO Ideas Godzilla set. That is a rumor, not an announcement. Treat it that way until LEGO says otherwise.

A word of caution when you search: many pages that rank for "LEGO Black Friday 2026" are last year's coverage or placeholders. Check the date on anything before you trust a price.

Retiring sets: the last big sale, maybe

Some retirement guides suggest Black Friday may be the last major sale for sets expected to retire at the end of the year. Brickranker's tracker lists the UCS Millennium Falcon (75192) as expected to retire December 31, 2026, and Brick Fanatics has pointed to it leaving this year too. Retirement dates move often, though. LEGO has shuffled Star Wars retirement dates several times this year, so a date on a tracker is a forecast, not a promise.

If there is a set you want at a discount, Black Friday is a reasonable moment to buy it. If you miss it, the secondary market is where retired sets go next.

A plan that works

  1. Make your list now, with set numbers, so you can compare against the sale price instead of the hype.
  2. Check LEGO.com on November 21 if you are an Insiders member.
  3. Look at the retiring-soon sets on your list first. Those are the ones that may not come back around.
  4. For anything already retired, or for single minifigures and parts, skip the sale and check used. You pay for the figure you want, not a whole set.

The takeaway

Do not buy on rumor. Write your list now, wait for LEGO to confirm the offers, and use the nine-day window instead of racing the first hour.
