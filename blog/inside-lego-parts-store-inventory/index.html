Inside a 17,288-Piece LEGO Parts Store: What's Actually in Stock at Piece Pavilion

Piece Pavilion · November 5, 2026 · 4 min read

Piece Pavilion's BrickLink store holds 3,867 lots and 17,288 individual pieces as of this morning's inventory pull. Of those lots, 1,340 are new and 2,517 are used. Most stores show you a catalog; here is what the shelves actually look like, and what it means for how you should shop for parts.

The shelves are mostly used, and mostly small lots

Used lots outnumber new ones almost two to one (2,517 versus 1,340). That tracks with how a parts store gets stocked: we sort bulk and set teardowns, so used parts arrive faster than factory-sealed ones. It also means you will often find colors and elements that LEGO no longer makes new.

Lot size is the other pattern. 2,856 of our 3,867 lots have two or fewer pieces left. If you are after a specific element, a low-stock listing is normal, not a warning sign. If you need 20 of something, check quantity before you add anything to the cart, and ask us about combining lots.

Plates, bricks and slopes are the backbone

By lot count, the biggest categories in the store are:

  • Plate: 375 lots
  • Brick: 271 lots
  • Plate, Round: 221 lots
  • Slope: 191 lots
  • Plate, Modified: 179 lots
  • Tile, Round: 157 lots
  • Brick, Modified: 136 lots
  • Minifigure, Utensil: 135 lots

The takeaway for builders: if your design leans on basic plates, bricks and slopes, you will find the most variety in color and size here. Modified plates and bricks (the ones with clips, studs on the side and handles) are where the detail comes from, and there are a lot of them too.

Round bricks hold the most value per lot

Ranked by listed value rather than lot count, the top category is Brick, Round, with 72 lots. It beats Brick, Modified (136 lots) and Plate, Modified (179 lots) on dollars. Round elements are popular for towers, columns, wheels and anything that needs to look smooth, and they are easy to run short of in a specific color. Buy what you need for the whole build at once.

712 lots have been on the shelf over 180 days

That is the number we watch most closely. Aging lots are the ones we are most likely to discount, bundle or move, so if you want a deal, browse the older listings first. Fresh arrivals sell fastest, so if you spot something you need in a new listing, do not wait.

How to shop this inventory

  1. Start with the part number from your build instructions or the Product Finder, not the color.
  2. Pick used unless you need showroom-clean condition. For most builds, used is identical in function.
  3. Check quantity first. With 2,856 low-stock lots, you may need to combine two listings.
  4. Message us if the exact lot is not there. Our inventory changes daily.

Bottom line: this is a store built around small, specific lots of plates, bricks, slopes and tiles. Search by part number, buy the full quantity you need in one go, and look at older listings for the best prices.
