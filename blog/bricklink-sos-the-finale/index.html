BrickLink SOS: The Finale

Piece Pavilion · November 12, 2026 · 3 min read

We’re back. And there’s some good news for sellers.

Well, this is the update I’ve been hoping to write.

BrickLink is back online. After following the outage through this series, it’s good to finally have something more useful to share than another maintenance notice.

For those of us selling on BrickLink, there were plenty of questions. What happened? Was our information accessed? What about our inventory and orders? And when could we get back to business?

BrickLink has now answered a lot of those questions. There’s also a pretty significant bit of news for sellers: BrickLink seller fees are being waived for November and December.

Two months. Sellers won’t be invoiced for those fees for either month.

I’ll get back to that, because it deserves some attention. First, here’s what BrickLink actually told us.

What happened to our information?

BrickLink says it took the site offline after detecting unusual activity. Its investigation confirmed that some usernames and IP addresses were accessed, along with a very small number of email addresses.

So this part is confirmed. It isn’t just something people were speculating about during the outage.

BrickLink says its investigation found that passwords and payment information were not accessed. It also says it has emailed members whose usernames or email addresses were accessed, and its current guidance is that members don’t need to take any action.

Before reopening, BrickLink says it worked to contain the issue, investigate what happened, check for related problems and determine whether member information or accounts had been affected. It says it has addressed the issue and will continue monitoring the platform.

We still don’t have every technical detail. I’m going to leave those blanks alone rather than pretend we know more than we do.

The question I had as a seller

What about the inventory?

BrickLink says it found no evidence that store inventories, orders, wanted lists or messages were lost or changed because of the incident. That’s an important answer for anyone running a store.

If something does look wrong, BrickLink asks members to contact Support with as much detail as possible. That applies to accounts, inventory, orders or other parts of the site.

Some features may still be unavailable, too. The Studio Forum remains offline for now, with more information promised over the coming days.

About those seller fees

November and December seller fees are being waived as a gesture of appreciation for sellers’ patience during the disruption.

As a seller, I appreciate that. The timing matters, especially with holiday shopping and builds coming up. It doesn’t give anyone the lost days back, but two months without BrickLink seller fees is a welcome bit of relief.

To be clear, this is the BrickLink seller-fee waiver announced in its update. It isn’t a promise that shipping or payment-processing fees disappear.

Back to the bricks

And somewhere in all of those details is the part we’ve been waiting for: the marketplace is back, and we can get back to Piece Pavilion.

This series started with a lot of questions. We have more answers now, and I wanted this last update to stick to the information BrickLink has actually provided.

Thanks for following along with BrickLink SOS. Hopefully the next hard-to-find thing we’re looking for is a LEGO piece.

I’d much rather write about that.

Sources: BrickLink’s reopening announcement and incident FAQ.
