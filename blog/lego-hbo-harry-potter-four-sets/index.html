All 4 LEGO Sets From HBO’s Harry Potter

Piece Pavilion · November 28, 2026 · 2 min read

The first thing I noticed in LEGO’s new Gryffindor Common Room was the tiny Quidditch table. LEGO says it’s specific to the HBO series, and it’s the detail that made me want to look closer. There are four sets based on the show. The series starts at Christmas, and LEGO lists all four sets for January 1, 2027.

Gryffindor Common Room (76482)

$89.99 · 738 pieces. This is the one I’d pick. The Fat Lady guards the entrance, and the dorms are upstairs. The little Quidditch table is the best part: LEGO says it’s unique to the show. You also get Parvati Patil and Lavender Brown alongside Harry, Ron and Hermione.

Privet Drive (76481)

$59.99 · 545 pieces. The two floors separate so you can get to Dudley’s bedroom and the cupboard under the stairs. All three Dursleys are here with Harry. If you watched the teaser, this is the house it spends time in right at the start.

Hogwarts Express & Platform 9¾ (76487)

$99.99 · 771 pieces. The train is the big draw, but the station gets a café, a waiting room and a luggage cart, too. The carriage roof lifts off so you can seat the characters. It’s the priciest of the four.

Harry, Draco and Hagrid Figures (40992)

$24.99 · 380 pieces. The cheapest set gets you three BrickHeadz figures. Look for the Hogwarts sweaters on Harry and Draco and the pink umbrella on Hagrid. It’s the easy pick if you want something small for a shelf.

Preorder check: As of November 28, LEGO U.S. was taking preorders for Privet Drive and the Gryffindor Common Room, both shipping from January 1. The Express and the BrickHeadz trio were still marked Coming Soon. If you’re shopping before Christmas, these are January builds.
