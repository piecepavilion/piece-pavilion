BrickLink SOS Part 5: The Wait Continues

Piece Pavilion · November 9, 2026 · 3 min read

BrickLink is still down, and the wait has been extended again. The latest official notice says the site is expected to remain unavailable until at least Wednesday, November 11, at noon Eastern (EST). That is 9 a.m. Pacific (PST), or 6 p.m. Central European Time (CET).

The important words are “at least.” Wednesday at noon is the earliest time in the current notice, not a promise that everything will be working then. The estimate has already changed during this outage, and it could change again.

Status checked November 9, 2026.

If you just want the simple version of what is happening, this is it. No long list of theories, and no technical deep dive. BrickLink is unavailable, buyers and sellers are stuck waiting, and there are still questions the notice does not answer.

The BrickLink notice in the screenshot supplied for this update. It shows the Wednesday estimate; check the live notice for any later changes.

What is happening?

Opening BrickLink currently leads to its maintenance page instead of the marketplace. The message is short: “BrickLink is temporarily unavailable.” Below it are the Wednesday estimate and an apology for the disruption.

This is a wider marketplace outage, rather than just a problem with one seller's store. Normal shopping and account access are disrupted. Buyers cannot use the site as usual to browse stores, place orders or check order details. Sellers cannot count on their normal online tools for managing those orders and their inventory.

That makes even small jobs difficult. Looking up a part for a build, checking a purchase or getting an order ready can turn into another visit to the same holding page. A working internet connection on your end will not fix an unavailable marketplace.

Wednesday at noon, at the earliest

The official notice gives the time as 18:00 CET / 09:00 PST on Wednesday, November 11. For readers on U.S. Eastern time, that is 12 noon EST, not midnight.

Earlier estimates pointed to Friday and then Monday. Brick Fanatics' November 9 update reports the latest extension to Wednesday. So if you checked over the weekend hoping things would be back on Monday, the wait is now longer.

Plan around the possibility of more downtime. If the site returns at the listed time, great. If work takes longer, that “at least” leaves room for another delay. The clearest sign of recovery will be the marketplace actually working again.

What we still do not know

The current notice does not explain the technical cause. It also does not tell us exactly what is happening behind the scenes or provide a guaranteed reopening time.

It is easy to find guesses online, especially when an outage lasts this long. But a maintenance page is not proof of a hack, deleted inventory or lost orders. There is no need to turn a short update into a confident explanation we cannot verify.

What to do while we wait

For buyers, keep your order confirmations and payment receipts. If you already have a tracking link, check that directly. An inaccessible order page does not tell you whether a package has shipped. Avoid placing or paying for the same order again just because you cannot see its status.

For sellers, keep existing backups and make a note of any sorting, packing or inventory work done offline. When access returns, check those notes against the live records before repeating updates.

For now, the update is simple: BrickLink remains unavailable, and Wednesday at noon Eastern is the earliest estimate, not a guaranteed return. Check the official notice before making plans around it. Until the marketplace is working again, we are still waiting.
