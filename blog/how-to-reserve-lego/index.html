How to Reserve a LEGO Set, Minifigure, or Part at Piece Pavilion

Piece Pavilion · June 12, 2026

It happens to every builder: you spot the perfect retired set, the minifigure that completes your display, or that one part your MOC has been waiting for — but it's three days before payday, or you want to add a few more things to the order first, or the birthday it's meant for is still a month away. You close the tab, plan to come back… and someone else buys it.

There's a better way. We'll reserve it for you. Any set, any minifigure, any part in our store — held with your name on it, free, no deposit.

What Reserving Means

BrickLink (where our store lives) has a built-in reservation feature for sellers. When you ask us to reserve an item, we mark that exact listing as reserved for your BrickLink username. From that moment:

  • Only you can buy it. The item stays in our store, but it's locked to your account — nobody else can check out with it.
  • The price is the price. Whatever it listed for when you reserved it is what you pay. No surprises.
  • You check out on your schedule. Add it to your cart and complete the order whenever you're ready within the hold window.

All you need is a free BrickLink account — if you don't have one yet, our beginner's guide to buying on BrickLink walks you through it in a few minutes.

How to Reserve an Item (3 Steps)

  • 1. Find what you want. Browse our store and note the item name or number — or just copy the listing link.
  • 2. Send us a message. Email piecepavilion@gmail.com or message us on BrickLink with the item and your BrickLink username.
  • 3. We confirm, you relax. We'll flag the item as reserved for you and reply to confirm. Check out whenever you're ready — it'll be waiting.

What Reserving Is Great For

  • Payday timing — Lock in a rare find today, pay when the paycheck lands.
  • Combining shipping — Reserve items as you find them over a week or two, then check out once and pay one shipping charge instead of three.
  • Gifts — Spotted the perfect set in June for an August birthday? Reserve it now and order when you're ready to wrap.
  • Works in progress — Building a MOC and collecting parts as the design evolves? Reserve the hard-to-find pieces so they're still there when the design is final.
  • One-of-a-kind items — Most of our minifigures and sets are single-quantity. When it's gone, it's gone — reserving is how you make sure it isn't.

The Ground Rules

  • It's free. No deposit, no fee, no obligation.
  • We hold reserved items for up to 2 weeks. After that, the hold expires and the item goes back on the shelf. Need a different window — for a gift date or a bigger combined order? Just say so when you ask; we're a family store, we'll work with you.
  • First come, first reserved. Items are reserved in the order requests arrive.
  • Changed your mind? No problem — tell us and we'll release the hold, no hard feelings. (If a hold quietly expires, the item simply goes back on the shelf.)

Why We Offer This

Big marketplaces don't hold things for you — carts get sniped and that's that. We're a family-owned store, and reserving is exactly the kind of thing a neighborhood shop does that a warehouse can't. If holding a minifigure for a week means it ends up in the hands of the builder who really wanted it, that's a win for everyone.

See something you love?

Browse the store, then email us the item and your BrickLink username — we'll hold it with your name on it.
