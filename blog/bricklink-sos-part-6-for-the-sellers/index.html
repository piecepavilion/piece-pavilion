BrickLink SOS Part 6: For the Sellers

Piece Pavilion · November 10, 2026 · 4 min read

This one is for the sellers waiting to get back into their stores. If you have orders to check, parts to list or buyers waiting for answers, an unavailable marketplace can put ordinary work on hold. Having stock ready does not help much when you cannot reach the records you need.

As of November 10, 2026, BrickLink's official notice still says the site is expected to remain unavailable until at least Wednesday, November 11, at noon Eastern (EST). That is 9 a.m. Pacific (PST) or 6 p.m. Central European Time (CET). It is an earliest estimate, not a guaranteed reopening.

Status checked November 10, 2026. The seller steps below are practical suggestions from Piece Pavilion, not new BrickLink rules or assurances about your account.

The work does not disappear when the site goes down

For a seller who cannot access their store, the problem can go beyond missing new orders. You may need to confirm a payment, double-check a shipping address, look up an order's contents or answer a buyer's question. If the only copy of that information is inside the unavailable site, that job may have to wait.

Some sellers may have saved invoices, order emails, exports or packing lists to work from. Others may not. The impact depends on your records and what stage each order has reached. There is no verified figure here for lost sales, and the maintenance notice does not tell us how any individual store or order is affected.

If your normal routine has stalled, that is frustrating enough without feeling you have to solve everything today. Start with what you can verify.

Protect the orders already in progress

Make a simple list of the orders you know about from records you already have. Keep the order number, payment evidence, packing progress and any tracking number together. Leave anything you cannot confirm marked as unknown rather than filling in the blanks from memory.

  • Already shipped? Keep the postage receipt and tracking details. Check the carrier directly if you need a delivery update.
  • Packed but not shipped? Keep the parcel labeled and separate. Before dispatching, confirm the contents, payment and destination from reliable records.
  • Missing essential details? Avoid guessing an address, substituting parts without agreement or assuming a payment went through. Wait until you can verify what is needed.

If a buyer contacts you through an existing channel, explain what you can confirm about their order. Keep the site's estimated return separate from any dispatch promise you make. Do not promise Wednesday shipping solely because Wednesday is in the maintenance notice.

Keep offline stock changes easy to trace

Sorting and checking parts can be useful while you wait, but give yourself a record of anything that changes. Note the part, color, condition, quantity and storage location. Keep newly sorted stock separate from stock already counted in your online inventory until you can reconcile it.

If you sell the same physical stock elsewhere, record those sales too. When BrickLink returns, compare your records before relying on the old quantities or uploading changes. Preserve an untouched copy of your last saved export or backup rather than overwriting your only reference.

You do not need to reorganize the whole store. A tidy packing area, checked supplies and clear notes may be more useful than starting a large inventory change you cannot finish safely.

When access returns, check before rushing

Work through your existing orders first. Compare payment, packing and shipping records with what the store shows. Then reconcile inventory changes before making new promises or repeating uploads. If something does not match, investigate that difference instead of assuming the outage caused a loss.

The current notice does not explain the technical cause or announce seller fee waivers, deadline extensions or compensation. Do not budget or plan around those things unless BrickLink confirms them.

For now, the facts remain limited: BrickLink is unavailable, and noon Eastern on November 11 is the earliest time in its posted estimate. For sellers, the practical goal is to protect the work already done and leave clear records for the restart. Take it one order and one tray of parts at a time.
