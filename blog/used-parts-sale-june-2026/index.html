10% Off All Used Parts Through June 30 — and Why Smart Builders Shop the Sale

Piece Pavilion · June 19, 2026

Good news for builders: from now through June 30, every used LEGO part in our store is 10% off. No code to hunt for — the discount is already baked into the prices you see when you shop. It's a great moment to stock up, and below we'll make the case for why a parts sale is worth acting on (and how to squeeze the most out of it).

Why a parts sale matters more than it sounds

Parts are where a build's cost quietly adds up — a few cents here, a dollar there, multiplied across dozens or hundreds of pieces. That's exactly why a discount on parts hits differently than a markdown on a single set: the more your build needs, the more 10% actually saves you. A 300-piece MOC order or a bulk color top-up turns a small percentage into real money. And remember, used parts are already the most affordable way to build (here's why used LEGO is worth buying) — this sale stacks right on top of that.

The case for buying while the sale is on

It's easy to bookmark a deal and forget it. Here's why this one's worth acting on now rather than "later":

  • The window is short. Store-wide parts sales aren't an everyday thing — when it ends June 30, prices go back to normal.
  • You'll need the parts anyway. If a build is already on your list, buying the pieces during the sale is simply paying less for something you were going to get regardless.
  • Prices on LEGO tend to drift up, not down. As elements get used in fewer sets and stock thins out, individual parts can get pricier and harder to find. Buying on sale today beats paying full price for a scarcer part later.
  • It's the cheapest time to stock your staples. Plates, slopes, the connectors and clips you always run out of — your parts bin will thank you.

Shop smart: make the savings count

A few quick moves to get the most out of the sale:

  • Make your list first. Jot down the parts your next build (or your dwindling bin) actually needs, then shop with purpose. Our product finder makes a specific part easy to track down by name, color, or number.
  • Buy it all in one order. Consolidating into a single order means the sale price and one shipment instead of several — savings on top of savings.
  • Mind the colors. If your build needs a specific color, grab enough now — exact colors are the parts most likely to be hard to find later.
  • Stock the staples. 1x2 plates, common slopes, tiles, clips, and pins are never wasted. A sale is the time to top them up.
