Somebody Once Told Me… LEGO Is Making Shrek Minifigures

Piece Pavilion · July 27, 2026

The swamp is coming to your brick shelf. LEGO has officially revealed a Shrek Collectible Minifigures Series (set 71053) — a full dozen characters from the beloved films, arriving September 1, 2026 at $4.99 a bag. Collectible minifigures are our whole world here at Piece Pavilion, so this one has our attention. Here's the complete lineup and how to actually get the figures you want.

The full 12-character lineup

Like every LEGO collectible-minifigures series, these come in sealed blind bags — you don't know which one you're getting until you open it. The roster leans all the way into the Shrek universe:

  • Shrek
  • Fiona — paired with Donkey (a two-for-one bag)
  • Puss in Boots
  • Lord Farquaad
  • The Fairy Godmother
  • Prince Charming
  • The Dragon
  • Gingy (the Gingerbread Man)
  • Pinocchio
  • The Big Bad Wolf
  • Merlin
  • Thelonious

Most figures come with at least one themed accessory, and there's a fun bonus for completists: gather the whole series and you also unlock the Three Blind Mice. A full case typically holds three complete sets of the twelve.

The blind-bag problem (and how to beat it)

Here's the catch every minifigure collector knows: blind bags are a lottery. Buy a handful and you'll pull three Donkeys and no Puss in Boots. Buy a full case to guarantee the set and you've spent well over a hundred dollars — with a pile of duplicates left over. Feeling the bags through the packaging used to be the trick, but LEGO's boxes make that harder every year.

The collector's answer is simple: buy the exact figures you want as singles. Once the series is out, individual characters hit the secondary market, and you can pick up just the Fairy Godmother, just Gingy, or just the one you're missing — no duplicates, no luck required. That's exactly what a minifigure-focused BrickLink store is for. We plan to stock Shrek singles, so you can browse our minifigure collection and grab only what your shelf needs.

New to chasing collectible minifigs? Our guide on how to collect LEGO minifigures walks through building a set the smart way, and the most valuable minifigures shows why the rarer figures in a series can climb in value once it retires.

A big year for minifigure fans

Shrek is riding a genuine wave of licensed minifigure drops. Just last week we covered Olivia Rodrigo's debut LEGO collection and its five new figures — and now a full ogre-sized dozen lands in September. Whether you're after a display row of villains, that grinning Gingy, or the whole swamp gang, it's a fun stretch to be a minifigure collector.

Better out than in, as they say. Pick your favorites, skip the duplicate pile, and we'll help you complete the set.
