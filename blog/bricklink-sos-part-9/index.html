BrickLink SOS Part 9

Piece Pavilion · November 23, 2026 · 2 min read

On Friday, BrickLink said it was aiming to reopen on Tuesday, November 24, if the team could do it safely. In its November 23 update, BrickLink said it made progress over the weekend and was working through the steps still needed to bring the site back. Then came the line that stood out to me: “We’re not quite ready to confirm when access will be restored.”

That’s a carefully worded sentence. BrickLink didn’t say Tuesday was off the table, but it didn’t say Tuesday was still the plan either. There’s no new reopening date to replace it. And if you check the FAQ and still see November 24 as the target, you’re not imagining things. That earlier target is still there, but Monday’s statement stops short of backing it up.

If you’re waiting on an order, that uncertainty matters. BrickLink says an order that was already processed and packed may still ship, while one that hadn’t been processed may have to wait until the site comes back. If something can’t wait, check your original order or shipping email for contact information. The updated FAQ also says BrickLink found no access to passwords, payment details, bank account information, or national ID details during this incident. That’s good to hear, but it’s not the same as saying no personal information was accessed at all.

BrickLink says it’ll email members when the site is back, so you don’t have to spend your day refreshing the page. Tomorrow, Tuesday, November 24, is when BrickLink plans to give us another update. It’s not a promise that we’ll be back to buying and selling by then. We’ll share what BrickLink actually says tomorrow, whether that’s a reopening, a new target, or just more waiting.
