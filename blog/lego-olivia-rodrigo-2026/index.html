Olivia Rodrigo Gets Her Own LEGO Sets — and 5 Brand-New Minifigs Drop August 1

Piece Pavilion · July 21, 2026

Pop's biggest young star just made LEGO history. On August 1, 2026, Olivia Rodrigo becomes the first music artist ever to get her own line of LEGO sets — five of them — along with five brand-new minifigures based on her most iconic looks. Whether you're here for the vinyl display or you collect minifigs (that's our world), this drop is a big deal. Here's the full lineup, and a collector's-eye take on why the little figures might matter most.

“There are so many pieces of my world inside these sets — little nods to songs, memories, outfits and moments that mean a lot to me.” — Olivia Rodrigo

The full lineup, out August 1

Four of the five sit under the new LEGO Editions banner, joined by a first-of-its-kind Botanicals set. Pre-orders for the first three opened back in June, and everything lands August 1 at LEGO.com, LEGO Stores, and select retailers:

  • 43028 Vinyl — 360 pieces, $34.99. A buildable record display celebrating hits across all three albums, packed with hidden easter eggs. Includes a minifig in her SOUR-tour look.
  • 43029 Concert Moon — 670 pieces, $49.99. Inspired by the viral giant-moon moment from the GUTS world tour, with hidden drawers and concert details.
  • 43030 Secret Storage — 1,085 pieces, $79.99. A red guitar, a red megaphone, and a lyric notebook full of hidden compartments — plus an exclusive minifig tucked inside the TV, styled after the “good 4 u” cheerleader look.
  • 43031 Dual Guitar — 1,228 pieces, $119.99. The biggest in the range: a split acoustic-and-electric guitar hiding stage and backstage scenes, and it comes with two minifigures.
  • 11507 Flower Bouquet (LEGO Botanicals) — 400 pieces, $49.99. The first-ever artist-themed Botanicals set, with symbolic blooms, nods to Olivia's Filipino heritage, and a striking purple flower built from electric guitars.

Five minifigs — the part collectors will chase

Sets come and go, but for a lot of us the real hook is the minifigures. This collection introduces five unique Olivia Rodrigo figures, each with two facial expressions and stage outfits pulled from her SOUR, GUTS, and recent-era performances. The SOUR-tour figure alone has dual-moulded legs with side printing, a dual-sided head, and a brand-new hairpiece — the kind of custom detailing that makes a fig stand out on a shelf.

Here's the collector angle: these are licensed, launch-only exclusives. Figures that appear in just one or two sets — especially one hidden inside a larger, pricier build like Secret Storage — are exactly the kind that get harder to find once the sets retire. We've written before about what makes a minifigure valuable and how to collect them smartly, and this drop checks a lot of those boxes: real-world licensing, limited appearances, and heavy fan demand from outside the usual LEGO crowd.

None of that is a guarantee — plenty of figures stay affordable for years. But if you're a fan and a collector, the low-cost way in is the $34.99 Vinyl set: cheapest entry, its own dedicated minifig, and an easy display piece.
