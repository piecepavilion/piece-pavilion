The World Cup Is Here (and at Gillette) — LEGO Ways to Celebrate

Piece Pavilion · June 18, 2026

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is underway across the U.S., Canada, and Mexico — and for once, the world's biggest tournament is happening right in our backyard. LEGO noticed too: the brand rolled out a full lineup of World Cup–themed sets for the occasion. Whether you scored tickets or you're watching from the couch, here's how to get in on it, brick by brick.

Seven matches are being played down the road

Gillette Stadium in Foxborough — about 30 minutes south of Boston, and rebranded "Boston Stadium" for the tournament — is hosting seven World Cup matches between June 13 and July 9, 2026. That includes five group-stage games (with the likes of France, England, Scotland, Morocco, and Norway passing through), a Round of 32 knockout match on June 29, and a quarterfinal on July 9. For a LEGO shop based in Massachusetts, having the World Cup this close is a pretty fun summer.

LEGO went all-in for the World Cup

This year LEGO launched a wave of LEGO Editions football sets built around four of the game's biggest names — Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Kylian Mbappé, and Vinícius Júnior. The headliner, 43018 Lionel Messi – Celebration, is a 1,427-piece piece of 3D wall art of Messi in his iconic point-to-the-sky pose ($199). There are also "Soccer Highlights" dioramas, larger poseable buildable figures — and, for the first time ever, each player as an official LEGO minifigure, plus a U.S. National Soccer Team minifigure set. If you want any of those brand-new, LEGO's own shop is the place; if you're after the older or harder-to-find soccer pieces down the line, that's where a store like ours comes in.

Build your own World Cup — no $200 set required

The best part of LEGO is you don't need the official set to join the party. A handful of ideas that take an afternoon and a bin of bricks:

  • Fly your colors. Grab plates and bricks in your country's colors and build its flag, a scarf, or a banner for the shelf.
  • Build a mini pitch. A green baseplate, two tiny goals, and a white-tile field — instant tabletop stadium. Add stands and you've got a MOC.
  • A trophy build. Gold and tan pieces make a surprisingly convincing little trophy for your winning side.

Browsing individual parts and colors is exactly what our store is built for — pick the exact bricks your build needs instead of buying a whole set for a few pieces.

Assemble your own crowd of fans

No stadium is complete without a roaring crowd. LEGO's collectible minifigure series are full of costume "fans" — lions, parrots, peacocks and more — that make a perfect brick-built supporters' section. (The Lion Costume Fan is practically begging to cheer on England's Three Lions.) Build out a row of seats, fill it with characters, and you've got the atmosphere to match the match. Start your squad in our minifigures collection.

However you're celebrating this summer — at Gillette or on the couch with the group-stage on — there's a brick-built way to fly your flag. Pick your side, grab your pieces, and build it.
