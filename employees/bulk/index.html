Every sale traced back to the bulk buy it came out of — by the location tag in the lot's BrickLink remarks.

Tag a lot B-4 or B-4/F in remarks and it belongs to bin 4 (sub-bin F). BrickLink returns those remarks on every sold order line, so each sale attributes back to its bin — that's real history, not an estimate. Bins roll up to a purchase via the Bulk Bins column on the Purchases sheet — that's the only place cost is entered, and the only thing standing between you and profit per buy.
% sold on the bar is by value (revenue ÷ revenue + what's still on the shelf at our ask); the pieces count is shown underneath.
