MAKE IT YOURSNeed a little inspiration?

You choose the story. Here are a few directions to try:

  • Behind the scenes: show how you sort, clean, or pack an order.
  • A build diary: share the idea, a tricky step, and the finished result.
  • Collector’s picks: introduce a favorite set, minifigure, or unusual piece.
  • A news update: explain what changed, why it matters, and what readers can do next.

These are prompts for you to write from, not generated articles. Your text stays yours.

Cover photo

JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF. Maximum 8 MB each. Save adds uploads to the article. Uploaded image links are public if shared.

Tell a story with your photos
  • Cover: choose one clear image that introduces the story. Wider photos work well beside the headline.
  • Details: add a close-up, a work-in-progress shot, and the finished result below the article.
  • Captions: tell readers something the image cannot show on its own.
  • Descriptions: describe what is visible for readers using a screen reader.

The preview shows your layout. Review the text and photos before using Save & publish.
