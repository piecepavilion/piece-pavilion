Where to buy retired LEGO sets you can trust

When LEGO retires a set, it stops being made — but it lives on in the secondhand market, often climbing in value. The catch is knowing the set is complete and fairly priced. We hand-check our sets and describe condition honestly, so a "retired" listing is something you can actually trust.

Not sure what a set is worth? Read our guide on where to buy retired LEGO sets and get a fair price before you buy.

New, used, and complete sets

We carry sets in a range of conditions — sealed, complete used, and partial. Every listing states exactly what you are getting. If you are weighing the trade-offs, our post on whether used LEGO is worth buying breaks down when new vs. used makes sense.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your retired LEGO sets complete?

Each listing states the exact condition — sealed, complete, or partial. We hand-check used sets and describe them honestly so there are no surprises.

How do you price retired sets?

We price against BrickLink's price guide, which reflects real recent sale prices. Our goal is fair pricing, not the highest possible markup.

Do retired sets come with the box and instructions?

It varies by listing and is always noted. If the box or instructions are included, the listing will say so.

Can you help me find a specific retired set?

Often, yes. Email piecepavilion@gmail.com with the set name or number and we'll let you know if we can source it or consign one for you.
